# Makishima Shelf

White reading room, ink-black **Cormorant** typography, no Sibyl HUD. Projects are **book spines** on pale wood shelves; featured titles sit on the **upper shelf** only.

Click a spine to open a lectern panel (description, thumbnail, **Visit** only). Search and section filter hide spines and empty shelves. Palette: `#f7f5f0` paper, muted leather spine tones, slow ceiling light — unsettling calm.
