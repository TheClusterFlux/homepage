# Seraph Static (Concept L)

Retro UHF receiver layout: a **channel sidebar** (zero-padded CH numbers) drives a single **curved CRT viewport**, not Egoist Signal’s diagonal spine or bento grid.

Tuning plays a brief **canvas static burst** and VHS tracking band over scanlines and vignette. Featured projects sit in a horizontal **preset memory** strip that jumps the tuner.

Palette: void black, phosphor magenta, cyan band labels, amber channel digits — Guilty Crown broadcast mood without copying Concept F chrome.

Data from `/data/projects.json`; thumbnails from production CDN. Search, category filter, featured row, Visit-only links. Preview on port **8099** (`data-visual-theme="seraph-static"`).
