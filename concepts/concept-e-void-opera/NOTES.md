# Concept E — Void Opera

Dark theatrical opera with funeral-parlor gravity: velvet curtains frame a void stage, footlights glow at the lip, and slow smoke drifts through the pit. Featured projects stand under explicit spotlight cones; the archive reads as a vertical evening playbill with Cinzel Decorative act labels and Cormorant body copy.

Layout is proscenium-first (not a card grid): box-office search and genre filter, a principal-performance row, then a single-column act list with roman numerals and gold rules. Visit is the only outbound link. Thumbnails load from the production CDN. Local preview on port 8094 via `node concepts/run-concepts.js`.
